## Why this exists

The first screen, and the entry point: until now every loop has been started from a terminal. **D21 says nothing in
the demo may require one**, and this is where that begins to be true.

## Scope

### The list

Every loop, with status, blueprint, the lane it is on, and total cost through T4's renderers. Ordered by most
recently active.

`LoopCard` ports from v1 (T5). Its status tone mapping needs the statuses S8 actually produces — `running`,
`waiting`, `blocked`, `done` — and **`waiting` and `blocked` must not look the same**. One needs a person; the other
needs a decision about the system. Rendering them alike sends someone to fix a problem when they were only being
asked a question.

### Live, not polled (U3, D12, I4)

The list updates from the event channel. `loop.started`, `loop.advanced`, `loop.waiting`, `loop.blocked` and
`loop.finished` (S8's T11) each move a row without a refetch.

**No interval timer.** Two update paths disagree, and the disagreement shows as a UI flickering between states.

### Starting one

A blueprint picker and a prompt. The picker lists what the daemon has — never a hardcoded list, since blueprints are
data (L1) and the whole point is that someone can add one.

**A blueprint that fails validation is offerable but marked**, with its diagnostics reachable. Hiding it would leave
someone wondering why the file they wrote does not appear; showing it silently would let them start a loop that
cannot run. `--strict` failures are a warning here, not a bar: an `unverifiable` blueprint runs fine until it reaches
the unimplemented action (S8's L13).

### Empty and error states (D19)

- **No loops** — say so, and say how to start one. A blank panel is indistinguishable from a broken one, which is
  the exact failure U12's generated types exist to prevent, and it must not be reintroduced by an absent empty state.
- **No blueprints** — name the directory that was searched.
- **Bridge error** — render T1's three variants differently (O2). "Failed to load" over a daemon domain refusal
  sends people to the wrong problem.

### Staleness (T3, D5)

While stale, rows keep their last-known values **and say when they were last confirmed**. They do not blank out, and
they do not keep spinning as though live.
