## Why this exists

Two thin screens in one task (R7). Both exist to render an uncertain answer honestly, which is why they belong
together.

## Scope — `Blueprints`

### Three counts, three renderings (D15)

```
  valid          34 checks
  unverifiable    9        cannot verify — code actions arrive at S16
  invalid         0
```

**`unverifiable` is styled as neither an error nor a pass.** Those are the two lies S8 built three states to avoid
(L12), and a UI is where they get told: a red badge makes a correct blueprint look broken, a green one makes an
unchecked claim look verified.

Each `unverifiable` row names **the missing registry and the slice that brings it**. "Cannot verify" is an annoyance;
"cannot verify — arrives at S16" is an answer.

A **strict** toggle: the same findings, a failing verdict. Not a re-run — S8's `--strict` changes the verdict, not the
diagnostics, and a UI that refetches on toggle implies otherwise.

### Diagnostics with paths (D6 of S8)

Every diagnostic shows its `Path` — `lanes[build].ladder[verify].sets` — and its `Hint` where there is one. **Render
from `Kind`, not by parsing `Msg`**: S8 made diagnostics machine-readable (O1) precisely so this screen would not
string-match, and message wording will change.

Every diagnostic, not the first (S8's L9). Stably ordered, as S8's O2 guarantees.

### The list

Blueprints the daemon has, with each one's verdict. The pinned content of a running loop is viewable from T7; this
screen shows the **files**, which is the other half of "why is this loop not doing what the file says".

## Scope — `System`

### Connection (T3)

The three liveness states, the daemon version, and the socket path. S1's window already showed version and connection;
this is that, kept rather than replaced.

### The recovery report (D14)

What recovery did on the last start, per step, with its case:

```
  recovered 1 of 2 · 1 failed
    4f2a1c   case A · tail complete · resumed
    9c14a2   bash receipt without completion — outcome unknown
             the step failed; nothing was re-run
```

**A failed step must name the tool and the receipt state.** *"It started and we cannot say what happened"* is a
different fact from *"it failed"*, and only one of them tells someone to go look at their repository. Render from S7's
structured `RecoveryFailure`, not its prose.

`RecoveryPanel` ports from v1 (T5) with its content replaced by that structure.

### What is not here

**No daemon controls.** No stop, no restart, no kill. R6's reasoning was about not handing an agent the means to
bypass guards, and the app is the human, so the argument does not forbid it — but it is **not in this slice's scope**
and adding it would be scope taken rather than given.
