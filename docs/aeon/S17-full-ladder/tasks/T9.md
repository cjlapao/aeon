# T9 — Walk the demo, and amend the QA suite

## Why this exists

**This is the first demo in the plan that is a product claim rather than a capability**, and it is the task that either
substantiates that or does not.

Set aside an hour. Most of it is waiting.

## Scope

### Walk every section, on a throwaway GitHub repository

**§2 is the section the slice exists for.** The plan's second task carries a **deliberate defect** — the `json` output
omits a field the spec required — and nothing is told about it.

**`verify` must catch it.** A ladder where `verify`, `security`, `review` and `qa` only ever pass has not been
demonstrated; they are decoration until one of them sends something back (L30).

Four things to confirm there, each a different property:

- The reject routed to **`rework: implement`**, not the top of the lane.
- The redo's input **contains the rejection's reason** — check `aeon step show --input`. A bare retry re-runs the same
  call that just failed.
- The counter went to **1 of 2**, not to the cap.
- **The other tasks' counters are untouched.** `aeon loop show --counters`, and read the table: this is where the
  two-level split is visible, and a per-loop-only implementation shows tasks 1 and 3 at 1 of 2 as well.

**If the defect is not caught, the fix is the handler or the criteria — never loosening the gate** (N8). A gate relaxed to
green a demo is worse than a red demo, because it ships.

**§3 forces `unverified`** by starving the linter of memory. Check the **stored** outcome:

```
aeon step show <verify step> --json | jq '.outcome'
```

**It must never read `pass`.** A linter that was killed found nothing **because it did not run**, and reporting that as
clean is the most plausible lie in the system — it looks like a green check.

**§4** — let a task exhaust its retries, confirm its siblings keep their budgets, then run the same thing unattended and
confirm the escalation becomes a **block** whose reason names **both** the cap and that nobody could be asked.

**§7 is the actual claim, and no test can substitute for it.** Read the merged PRs in order and reconstruct what happened
**without Aeon**: what was asked, what was planned, which task did what, what came back and why, what was deferred. **If a
competent person cannot, the method did not work regardless of what the loop reported.**

Record how long the whole run took and how many model calls it made. v1's QAC-33 asked for the same numbers and the reason
holds: that count is the hybrid decision's payoff.
