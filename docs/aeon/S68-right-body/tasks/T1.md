## Notes — what the task file could not have known

- **Deviation (1): policy renders LAST** in the system half. The spec
  sketched a fixed position; last won because every other section composes
  around the frozen contract, and the cap (32) plus ID-sort keeps I5.
- **Deviation (2): B9's fallback is a pinned sentence, not a provenance
  column.** `loop.ContinueFallbackText` is one constant; the M26 rule makes
  a provenance column on human rows wrong anyway (the transcript does not
  carry system-side provenance).
- **Deviation (3): the Task section carries no heading** — an empty Title
  is what routes it, and a heading would make the frame speak twice.
- **Deviation (4): `OpeningTranscript(h, systemText, transcriptText, history)`**
  is the exported shape (the spec implied a method on the result); it trims
  exactly ONE trailing renderer `\n` from the user block — a pin, because
  two would mean the renderer drifted.
- Role refusals name the offending role (AGENTS §5's refusal rule), and
  unknown roles from the wire pass through to runtime's refusal rather
  than being guessed at by the protocol layer.
