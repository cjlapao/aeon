# T4 · The daemon drain: shutdown that leaves steps recoverable

**Prefix:** LC · **Rules:** LC19 · **Depends on:** T3. Parallel-safe with T5–T8 after T3 merges. **Riskiest task of the slice — isolated on purpose.**

## Goal

At SIGTERM the daemon stops starting work, cancels in-flight loop work **with a typed cause**, terminates bash process groups, and leaves interrupted steps `running` at their last checkpoint for the existing recovery walk — instead of abandoning goroutines and children when the process exits. No step is ever committed as `failed` because the daemon shut down.

## Background (verified)

Runners start on `context.Background()` (`daemon/start_loop.go:122`, `runs.go:202`, `blueprint.go:287,308,375`); only `StartLoops(recoveryCtx)` passes a cancellable ctx (`blueprint.go:1009`). `serve.go:355-374` cancels only `recoveryCtx`, waits `shutdownGrace` (30 s) for recovery/automation, calls `d.Shutdown()` (terminals only, `daemon/terminal.go:50-57`) and `bootstrap.Shutdown`. A cancelled step context returns `failed` from the runtime (`runtime/loop.go:230-236`); the stop registry exists precisely because the generic cancellation cannot tell a stop from a drop (`daemon/stepcancel.go` header). `bash` children are `Setpgid` and killed on ctx cancel (`tools/spawn.go:128-160,236-243`).

## Files

- `aeon/internal/daemon/daemon.go` — a daemon-lifetime `loopCtx, cancelLoops := context.WithCancelCause(context.Background())` created at construction; every `d.loopRunner.Start(context.Background(), …)` site (the five above) passes `d.loopCtx` instead (a grep test asserts no `Start(context.Background()` remains).
- `aeon/internal/loop/runner.go` — `Drain()`: sets a draining flag; `Start` returns `false` once draining (no new runner); an in-flight runner finishes its current advance's cancellation per its ctx. `DrainWait(d time.Duration)` waits for `active` to empty.
- `aeon/internal/daemon/shutdown.go` — **new**: `d.Drain(ctx)`: (1) `loopRunner.Drain()`, (2) `cancelLoops(ErrDaemonShutdown)`, (3) `DrainWait(shutdownGrace)`; returns a `DrainReport{Runners, SettledSteps, LeftRunning}` logged as one line.
- `aeon/cmd/aeon/serve.go` — after `cancelRecovery()`/waits, call `d.Drain(shutdownCtx)` **before** `d.Shutdown()` and `bootstrap.Shutdown`.
- `aeon/internal/protocol/step.go` (the commit paths at `~569-653` and `~723-758`) and `aeon/internal/daemon/blueprint.go` (`RunStepForLoop`): when the run's context ends with `context.Cause(runCtx) == loop.ErrDaemonShutdown`, **do not persist a failed/cancelled outcome**: leave the step row `running` at its last checkpoint (the runtime saves a checkpoint every turn, so recovery has something to resume — verify in T0 §1.4), skip `FinishStep`, emit nothing but a log line. A `step.stop`'s `wasStopped` bit still wins (a person's stop during shutdown parks `suspended`/`user`).
- `aeon/internal/tools/spawn.go` — **no edit expected**: process-group termination already follows ctx cancel; the test proves it through the whole stack.

## Decision gate (OD7)

If T0 §1.4 shows the commit path cannot consult the cause without a wider change than the two sites above, **stop and apply the fallback**: keep `loopCtx` and `Drain` (so process groups are terminated and no goroutine outlives its ctx), but **do not** change what a step commit does; document in Notes that a step cancelled by shutdown is recorded as today and the recovery walk handles `running` rows only. The slice must never make recovery worse; the T1 shutdown golden is the arbiter.

## Do NOT
