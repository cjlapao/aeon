## Why this exists

**A slice is done when someone else can follow `DEMO.md` and watch it work.**

Three steps in this demo are harder to walk honestly than they read, and each has a way of appearing to
pass:

**§4 — the goroutine count.** `aeon debug goroutines | grep -c agentsvc` must be `0`. **If that command
does not exist, this step proves nothing** — and it is the step that protects P2. Build the diagnostic or
record that you asserted it another way.

**§5 — the partial rollup.** The total must read *"at least $0.0305"*. **If every child happens to be
`known`, the step passes without exercising P32 at all.** Confirm a child was genuinely `partial`.

**§6 — the cycle.** It must refuse as a **cycle**, not as a depth or cost failure. Those are easy to
confuse from the outside, and confusing them is the exact mistake AS7 exists to prevent.

**Read first:** `DEMO.md` in full; `docs/test_suites/aeon-agents.md`, which **exists** for S13 — amend it;
S11's note on asserting properties rather than counts.
