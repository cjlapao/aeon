# T6 · The UI: card, strip, load on open, divider

**Rules:** TD13–TD16. **Depends on:** T3.

## Steps

1. `components/agent-chat/types.ts`: a `ChatItem` kind `todo` ({items, counts, revision, active, closedBy?}) and `todoNudge` (divider). `fromEvents.ts`: map `todo.updated` to the card and `todo.nudged` to the divider; when replaying a transcript with no event, parse the `todo` tool result's render (`[ ] "id": content`, the counts line) into the same item. The existing generic `tool` row for `todo` is replaced by the card (keep the row for every other tool).
2. `items.tsx`/`cards.tsx`: the checklist card, with `[ ] / [~] / [x]` as icons and the counts line; one card per update, the latest expanded and earlier ones collapsed to their counts.
3. A pinned strip in `features/loop-workspace` above the composer: collapsed line `Todo · 3 of 5 done`, expands to the list, shows `closed (all done)` or `closed by you`, and a Close button (calls `loop.todos.close` through the typed bridge) while active and the connection is current.
4. On opening a loop the workspace asks `loop.todos` once, then follows `todo.updated` (same subscription the chat already holds). The strip is filled before any new event.
5. i18n keys in en and pt; the policy/tools guards forbid recalled vocabulary words in production sources, so the status words (`pending`, `in_progress`, `done`) come from the daemon's payload, not literals, where a guard applies (run the guards and fix, do not exempt).
6. Tests: the card from a `todo.updated` event and from a replayed result; the strip loads before any event; updates move it; close calls the method and the strip says closed; the divider.

## Definition of done

- Acceptance 1, 2 and 6 hold in tests; the full UI suite and `npx vite build` pass; measured counts in Notes.

## Do not

- Do not parse a tool result when the event exists.
- Do not invent a status word the daemon did not send.
