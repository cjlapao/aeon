## Context

This is the slice that completes Stage 1, so the demo is the first one a person might follow because they want
to **use** Aeon rather than verify it. That changes what the document owes them.

**§8 must not be softened.** It states that `bash` has no policy, that `read` is confined and `bash` is not, and
that the allow-list is the only boundary. It also holds the demo's most important negative check — a step
without the flag being unable to run a command.

Everything else about this slice works and invites use, which is exactly why §8 is the most consequential
paragraph in the plan so far. A demo presenting `--allow-bash` as a convenience would be actively misleading. If
the walk tempts you to trim it for flow, that is the signal to leave it alone.

**Two suites need amending**, and one of them needs a shape that is easy to get wrong. `aeon-tools.md` was
written for nine tools; seven do not exist yet. Those cases are marked **not yet applicable, with the slice
that will make them applicable** — not deleted. A deleted case is one nobody re-adds, and S11 would then ship
seven tools with no suite.

**Read first:** `DEMO.md` in full; both suites as they stand, written for TypeScript; the brief's §2.
