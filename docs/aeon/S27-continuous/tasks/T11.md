# T11 · End to end: restart, crash, runaway, fairness

**Prefix:** CN · **Rules:** CN8, CN9, CN11, CN12, CN14, CN23 · **Depends on:** T5, T6, T7.

## Goal

Prove, against a real daemon with a **deterministic fake model lane** and a fake clock, the behaviours that unit tests cannot: nothing starts twice across
restarts and kills, a blocked repository stays held for days of ticks, a runaway chain is bounded, fairness and aging hold under pressure, and the whole
pipeline — roadmap item to done — runs unattended with every decision legible.

## Scenarios (`internal/daemon/continuous_e2e_test.go`, build-tagged like the existing daemon e2e tests; each runs under `-race`)

1. **Roadmap to done, unattended.** A repository with the S56-less pin (false/false), three `ready` roadmap items A → B → C by dependency (A first, B waits on A, C on B),
   `maxAgentSlots=1`. Drive ticks: A starts; while A runs B/C show `dependency-open` in `scheduler.preview`; A finishes ⇒ kick ⇒ B starts; … all three `done`;
   `loop_run.started_by_kind='scheduler'` for each; `scheduler_decision` has the `started` rows with the grant revision (empty without S56).
2. **Queue before roadmap; attended items skipped.** A queue of [attended item, normal item] plus a ready roadmap item: the attended one reads `attended-item`, the normal one
   starts first, the roadmap item waits `queue-live`; after the queue drains the roadmap item starts.
3. **Capacity.** Five repositories, `maxAgentSlots=2`, weights 1: exactly two loops run; the other three read `deferred(capacity)` once each (dedupe); as loops finish, the
   next in CN11's order starts; a repository with `max_parallel_agents=3` and cap 2 is refused once `weight-exceeds-cap`.
4. **Aging.** A low-priority repository deferred beyond `agingHours` (fake clock) jumps ahead of a stream of fresh `known` items — no starvation over 200 ticks.
5. **Blocked holds.** A scheduler-started loop blocks (`model-error`): over 10,000 ticks nothing starts in that repository, other repositories are unaffected; a `needs-you` row
   exists once (dedupe) with kind `model-error`; the person cancels the queue item / answers ⇒ the repository frees and the next unit starts.
6. **Kill mid-pick.** Cancel the daemon after the claim and before the loop exists (a fault-injection hook at the start seam); restart: `Reconcile` starts it **once**; a
   second restart starts nothing; the loop's slug is the deterministic one. Kill **after** the loop exists but before the stamp: restart stamps, starts nothing.
7. **Refusal is final.** A blueprint removed after enable: the pick refuses `template-invalid`; ticks and restarts never retry; `needs-you` shows it.
8. **Runaway bounded.** An instantly finishing fake blueprint with 100 ready items and `maxStartsPerRootPerHour=6`: exactly 6 starts in the fake hour, `deferred(rate-limit)` with the leave-instant, the 7th starts when the oldest leaves the window.
9. **Window.** A Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00 Europe/Lisbon window across a DST change and a Saturday: no start outside, `window-closed` carries the next open instant, the first tick after opening starts.
10. **Pause and stop-all.** `pause` leaves running loops running and starts nothing; `resume` continues; `stopAll` (with a fake S55 stopper) pauses then stops each scheduler loop once.
11. **No inheritance of authority.** Enable a root's repositories via `enableTree`; attach a new child component with a repository; it is **not** enabled and never starts.
12. **A person starts a loop on an enabled repository** while the scheduler is idle: the scheduler yields (`repo-occupied`), the person's loop counts toward slots; when it finishes the scheduler resumes.
13. **Disabled and master-off byte-identical.** The reactor behaviour for disabled repositories equals T1's goldens with the worker running.
14. **Idle loops and `--no-recover`.** An idle-resting loop (`after: converse`) neither occupies nor is started; `--no-recover` ⇒ no scheduler.
