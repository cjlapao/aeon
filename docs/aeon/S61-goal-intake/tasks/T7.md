# T7 · Daemon methods, errors, conformance, and the human-door guard amendment

**Prefix:** GI · **Rules:** GI2, GI4, GI7, GI8, GI17 · P4, P34 · **Depends on:** T6.

## Goal

Everything on the socket, as a **human door**: charter, template and intake methods; typed refusals; the contract chain;
and sibling guards with canaries proving the writes are absent from every non-human surface and that the charter grants
nothing.
