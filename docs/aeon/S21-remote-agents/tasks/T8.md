# T8 — `cost.go`: unknown stays unknown

## Why this exists

**A remote agent's spend is not observable here**, and this is the easiest place in the whole product for
the cost design to quietly fail.

Because the field is **genuinely empty.** Locally, a missing cost is a bug worth chasing. Remotely it is
the normal case — the peer may not report usage, may not know it, may not wish to say.

**And an empty field rendered as `$0.00` is the precise failure the entire cost design exists to prevent**
(P31). *"Unknown"* becomes `—`, `—` becomes `0`, and a total that looks confident is wrong in the direction
nobody checks.

**So: `unknown` stays `unknown`** (RA10), and **the parent cannot be `known` while a child is unknown**
(P32) — an aggregate over uncertain values is itself uncertain.

**Read first:** S3's three-state `Cost`; P31 and P32 in
[`../../ARCHITECTURE.md`](../../ARCHITECTURE.md); S14's `cost.go` — **this extends its rollup, it does not
replace it**; the design's RA10.

## Scope

**1. A remote Task with no reported cost is `unknown`** — not zero, not omitted, not absent from the
rollup. It appears, and it says `unknown`.

**2. The reason is recorded.** *"The peer reported no usage"* is a different fact from *"we have not asked
yet"*, and a transcript read later cannot tell them apart otherwise.

**3. A reported remote cost is recorded with its provenance** — it is the peer's claim, not our
measurement. **If the peer's number is later shown to be wrong, the record should say whose number it was.**

**4. `unknown` never renders as a number.** Assert it, including inside a total — which is where it
reappears, because a sum needs an operand and `0` is the available one.

**5. The parent becomes less certain** (P32), through S14's existing propagation. **Do not add a second
rollup** — extend the subject.

- **Zero is the LOOK of free, and it OMITS.** Both number fields stay zero so the JSON has no `usd` key

- **The currency refusal is name-and-don't-count**: a claim in `eur` lands as unknown whose reason names

  enters.
