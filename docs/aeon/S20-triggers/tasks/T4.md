## Why this exists

**Two failures, and the second one is invisible.**

**The burst.** The naive reconcile loops over every missed instant and fires each one. A daily job missed
for a week starts seven loops; a minutely job missed for a week starts **ten thousand**. In production that
is a thundering herd, each loop believing it is today's. **At most one catch-up, never a burst** (TR3).

**The silence.** A missed fire that is simply absent from the log is **indistinguishable from one that
never should have happened.** So *"why didn't the nightly job run?"* has no answer, and the person asking
concludes the scheduler is unreliable — which it is, in the only sense that matters.

> ***"Did this fire?"* has three answers: fired · did not fire · we were down and cannot say.**

**P31's ninth instance**, and the third answer is the whole point of this task.

**Read first:** `clock.go` (T2); P31 in [`../../ARCHITECTURE.md`](../../ARCHITECTURE.md); the design's TR3,
TR4; S10's T11 for the three-state rendering precedent.

## Scope

**1. Reconcile on startup**, bounded to **one** fire — the most recent missed instant, if any.

**2. Record the rest as `could not say`.** Not as `did not fire` — the daemon being down is a different
fact from the window excluding it, and collapsing them loses the signal.

**3. A closed three-value type**, not two booleans. Two booleans admit a fourth combination that means
nothing, and somebody will render it.

**4. The three never collapse in rendering.** A test, not a convention — the failure is cosmetic and total,
exactly as S10's T11 and S9's cost renderers describe.

**5. Answer Q3: does a paused schedule record `could not say`?** **The design's answer is no** — paused
means it would not have fired, so there is nothing to be uncertain about. **State it**, because the
implementation falls out differently and a paused schedule accumulating uncertainty rows is noise that
hides the real ones.

**6. Recording ten thousand skipped instants must not be the slow part.** A week of a minutely schedule is
10,079 rows. **Either record a range or record the count** — and say which in the notes, because
row-per-instant will be noticed the first time somebody schedules something frequent.
