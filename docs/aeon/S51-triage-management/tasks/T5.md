  name; `agentSlug` tries `AgentBySlug` then `AgentByName` and REFUSES an id
  that names no agent row — identity is stamped AND real, filed_by lands the
  slug (T1's conversion made the slug canonical). `Identity.Agent` (stepctx),
  not the caller-passed actor, is the source (OL3's stamp).
- **The cap constant:** `triage.MaxOpenClaimsPerAgent = 8` (`internal/triage/types.go`).
  Eight, not S45's twenty: proposals are inert rows; a filed claim enters a
  human backlog people must work — TM6's own reasoning (filing is cheap,
  triaging is not). Counted by SQL (`Store.CountOpenClaimsByFiler`:
  `filed_by = ? AND validation_state = 'unvalidated' AND duplicate_of IS
  NULL`), refused BEFORE the write; a store that cannot count STOPS the write
  (the refusal says so) — the cap is never trusted to a failed count. The
  sentence names the agent, the number, the constant and `bug_list` as the way
  to see them.
- **The filing builds `NewBug` field by field** — title, body, `FiledBy: slug`,
  priority `triagePriorityState("")` (the honest empty, store default
  `unknown`); there is no map to smuggle through, and validation state is
  nobody's to choose (store default `unvalidated`, D1's backstop).
- **Person half:** the three pointer sentences ride `personDoorRefusal`
  (records_identity.go) — reads point at `triage.board`/`triage.show`
  (unscoped), the filing points at `triage.file` "the person's door… under
  you, not under a step (TM6)".
